About the numbers: elapsed days/hours/minutes/seconds and biological lifetime estimates are marked with ≈ where an exact birth time or biological rate is unknown.

Birth-day context: Ottawa weather, Canadian music-chart context, 1999 box-office context, Nunavut's creation, the UN six-billion milestone, and the Mars Climate Orbiter timing were checked against historical/reference sources during development.

Childhood-media ages: ages are based on Fiona's birthday, September 28, 1999, and the original premiere/release years of the listed shows and films. The games and music are included as shared childhood memories, not because their release dates define when they were played or heard.
